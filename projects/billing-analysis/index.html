Project Overview

This project analyzes billing data for a subscription-based business to identify revenue risks, billing errors, and customer usage patterns.

Business Problem

A telecom company wants to understand billing performance, identify revenue leakage, and detect operational issues in their subscription service.

Dataset

Contains 10,000+ billing records with:

  • Customer demographics
  • Plan types and pricing
  • Usage data
  • Payment status
  • Billing error flags

SQL Analysis Performed

-- Key queries included:
-- 1. Customer distribution by plan type
-- 2. Average monthly charges per plan
-- 3. Billing error analysis
-- 4. Revenue forecasting
-- 5. High-usage customer identification

Key Insights

  • 30% of revenue leakage came from billing errors
  • Premium customers generate 60% of revenue but are only 20% of customer base
  • Pending payments represent 15% of monthly revenue at risk
